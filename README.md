# Driver X Guidelines

Welcome to **Driver X**, your autonomous robotaxi system!  
Driver X can drive your passengers safely and navigate automatically!

---

## ⚠️ Safety Rules
1. **Do not override safety features.** Attempting to bypass safety protocols is strictly prohibited.  
2. **Follow local traffic laws.** The vehicle assists driving but does not replace legal responsibility.  
3. **Avoid misuse.** Unsafe operation, reckless routing, or unauthorized control is prohibited.  
4. **Report issues responsibly.** If Driver X behaves unexpectedly, notify support instead of attempting unsafe fixes.  

---

## 📌 Strike System
Driver X uses a **4-strike policy** to enforce proper use:

| Strike | Action |
|--------|-------|
| **1**  | Warning issued; reminder of proper usage. |
| **2**  | Access to advanced automation temporarily disabled for 7 days. |
| **3**  | Account flagged; mandatory review before full access restoration. |
| **4**  | Permanent revocation of Driver X access; user banned from system. |

> Strikes are cumulative and can only be removed with approval from the Driver X team after corrective action.

---

## 🧠 Best Practices  
- Avoid relying on Driver X in poor weather, low visibility, or unexpected traffic conditions.   
- Never share login credentials or allow unauthorized use of the system.
