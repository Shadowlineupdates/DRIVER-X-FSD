# Driver X Guidelines

Welcome to **Driver X**, your fully autonomous robotaxi.  
Driver X is capable of driving completely on its own — no human driver is required. However, **passengers must follow safety and usage guidelines** to ensure a safe ride.

---

## ⚠️ Passenger Safety Rules
1. **Stay seated and wear a seatbelt.** Driver X cannot compensate for unsafe passenger behavior.  
2. **Do not interfere with vehicle operation.** Do not attempt to open doors, override controls, or tamper with the system.  
3. **Follow local laws and policies.** Passenger behavior must comply with traffic rules and local regulations.  
4. **No unsafe or illegal use.** Misuse of Driver X (e.g., intentional reckless behavior, unauthorized cargo, or tampering) is strictly prohibited.  
5. **Report issues responsibly.** Notify Driver X support if something malfunctions; do not attempt to manually control or override the system.  

---

## 📌 Strike System
Driver X enforces a **5-strike policy** for passengers or authorized users:

| Strike | Action |
|--------|-------|
| **1**  | Warning issued for improper behavior. |
| **2**  | Certain features temporarily restricted (e.g., premium routes or vehicle amenities). |
| **3**  | Ride privileges temporarily suspended for 7 days. |
| **4**  | Account flagged; mandatory review before reinstatement. |
| **5**  | Permanent ban from Driver X services; access revoked. |

> Strikes are cumulative and may only be removed by approval from the Driver X team following corrective action.

---

## 🧠 Best Practices
- Remain seated and behave responsibly while in the vehicle.  
- Avoid carrying unsafe or prohibited items.  
- Keep your account credentials secure — unauthorized use is prohibited.  
- Report any unusual system behavior immediately.
